# Odoo Free Apps

Welcome to **Odoo Free Apps**! This is a place where I, [Edgardo Rios](https://github.com/edgardorios), share free and open-source Odoo apps that are simple, useful, and ready to enhance your Odoo setup. All apps are published on the [Odoo Apps Store](https://apps.odoo.com) and licensed under LGPL-3.

## Available Apps

### [Ribbon Banner](ribbon_banner/)
Adds a small customizable ribbon to the top-left corner of Odoo screens to help you identify the environment (like DEV, STAGING, or TEST) and avoid working in the wrong instance.

**Key Features:**
- Ribbon with environment name and database.
- Configure from **Settings > Ribbon Banner**:
  - Banner text
  - Background and text colors
  - Opacity

[Check it out on Odoo Apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/ribbon_banner/)  
![Ribbon screenshot](https://github.com/edgardorios/odoo-free-apps/raw/18.0/ribbon_banner/screenshots/banner.png)

### [Top Banner](top_banner/)
Adds a customizable two-line banner at the top of all Odoo screens. Perfect for displaying environment indicators, alerts, internal messages, or announcements.

**Key Features:**
- Two independent lines, each configurable with:
  - Background color
  - Text and text color
  - Optional hyperlink (text or button)
- Toggle the banner on/off from **Settings > Top Banner**
- Lines auto-hide if empty

[Check it out on Odoo Apps](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/18.0/top_banner/)  
![Top banner screenshot](https://github.com/edgardorios/odoo-free-apps/raw/18.0/top_banner/screenshots/homescreen.png)

## How to Install

Each app has its own `README.md` with installation and configuration instructions. Just open the app folder (e.g., `ribbon_banner/` or `top_banner/`) and follow the steps.

In general:
1. Copy the app folder into your Odoo addons directory.
2. Enable Developer Mode.
3. Update the Apps List.
4. Search for the app and click **Activate**.
5. Configure via **Settings**.
